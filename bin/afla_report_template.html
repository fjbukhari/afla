Runs of homozygosity

Long stretches where both copies of the DNA are identical. Their total length rises with parental relatedness; a recessive disease gene often lies inside one. Exome data give approximate boundaries and miss short runs.

Copy-number changes (CNVkit)

Log2 ratio of read depth against the reference (0 = normal, about −1 = one copy lost, +0.58 = one copy gained in a pure sample). Reference "pooled" = other samples of the run with the same kit, "user" = your reference, "flat" = no normals (only large, strong changes are believable). Exome/panel CNV calls must be confirmed by another method (e.g. MLPA, array, qPCR).

Structural variants and DNA fusions (Manta)

Capture panels and exomes see only breakpoints that fall inside or near targeted regions. A translocation (BND) between two genes is a candidate fusion; RNA or FISH confirmation is needed. "Pairs/split" are supporting read pairs and split reads.
